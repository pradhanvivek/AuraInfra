# Welcome to AuraInfra.ai Beta Testing! 🎉

Hi there,

Thank you for agreeing to be part of our exclusive beta testing program for **AuraInfra.ai** - your personal asset management platform!

## What is AuraInfra.ai?

AuraInfra.ai helps you manage and track all your valuable assets in one place:
- 🏠 Properties with documents, measurements, and AI floor plans
- 🚗 Vehicles with insurance and maintenance tracking
- 📱 Appliances with warranty reminders
- 💍 Jewelry with appraisal tracking
- 🪑 Furniture inventory
- 🎨 Art collection management
- 📊 Portfolio valuation and insurance reports
