AuraInfra Community Standards

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Community spaces are for respectful communication among authorized residents and administrators. By accepting these standards before posting or commenting, you agree to follow them.

Content that is not allowed

  • Harassment, bullying, hate speech, discrimination, threats, or encouragement of violence.
  • Sexually explicit material, exploitation, illegal activity, fraud, spam, or impersonation.
  • Private documents, contact information, financial details, visitor information, or photos of others shared without appropriate permission.
  • Content that infringes intellectual-property rights or intentionally misleads other residents.

Review before publication

New posts, comments, and edited content enter a review queue. A community administrator reviews the text and any attached photos before publication. Content without an approval status, including older content, remains hidden until reviewed. Approval does not prevent later removal.

Report, block, and get help

Use Report on a post or comment and explain your concern. Community administrators should review reports promptly and remove material that violates these standards. Use Block to hide the user's posts and comments and prevent viewing or interacting with each other's content. You can unblock an account from the community board.

Administrators can remove content and suspend a resident's access to the specific community. To escalate an unresolved report or contact AuraInfra directly, email support@aurainfra.ai. Include the community and a description of the concern; avoid unnecessary private information.
