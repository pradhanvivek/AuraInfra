AuraInfra Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

AuraInfra.ai provides personal asset management and community/HOA services. This policy describes the information handled by the app and its backend, why it is used, and how you can exercise your choices.

Information we handle

  • Account and authentication: username, email, optional phone and profile photo; password hashes for password accounts; provider identifiers for Google or Apple sign-in; app session identifiers and authentication times; short-lived hashed recovery codes for password resets. Apple may provide a private relay email. Apple refresh credentials used for account revocation are encrypted in our database.
  • Personal assets: property addresses and coordinates, asset descriptions, purchase prices, valuations, warranty and maintenance details, receipts, floor plans, photos, documents, and generated analyses that you choose to save.
  • Community membership: community, resident role, unit information, membership requests, verification documents, and administrator decisions. Uploaded approval documents are removed from the request after a decision.
  • Community activity: posts, comments, photos, likes, amenities and bookings, complaints, meetings and RSVPs, visitor names, phone numbers, invitation and check-in details, and community documents.
  • Payments: community dues and charges, amounts, currencies, payment status and processor references. Card entry occurs through the payment processor; the app does not ask you to enter card details into its own asset or community forms.
  • Security and diagnostics: request IP addresses used for abuse prevention and server error logs. The app does not intentionally include passwords or full uploaded documents in diagnostic logs.

How information is used and who can see it

We use information to authenticate accounts, manage assets, process membership requests, provide community services, support payments, respond to reports, and protect the service. Personal asset records are scoped to their owning account. Community information is shared with authorized community members or administrators as required by the feature. Community administrators can review membership documents, resident information, complaints, bookings, and moderation reports. Visitor lists are limited to the host or authorized security/administration staff. Platform administrators have privileged access to administer and support the service.

Posts and comments are reviewed before publication. Approved content is visible to authorized community members, subject to blocking. Do not post another person's private information without permission.

External services

  • Authentication: Google sign-in uses the configured Emergent authentication service to verify identity. Sign in with Apple verifies signed credentials with Apple and uses Apple's token service. These providers process sign-in information under their own policies.
  • AI analysis: after your consent, selected photos, receipts, floor plans, or text are sent through our backend to Google Gemini or OpenAI, directly or through Emergent's integration service, to generate analysis. Provider handling and retention depend on the service configuration and provider terms. We do not promise that third-party systems erase information immediately or exclude every use under their own terms. Manual entry remains available without AI. You can revoke permission in Profile.
  • Maps: Google Maps/Places and OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service receive address searches or coordinates when you use mapping features. External map directions open the selected map service.
  • Payments: Stripe receives information needed to process community payments. The processor and the relevant community may retain transaction information under their own obligations.
  • Hosting: our configured application hosting and database services process the information necessary to operate the app.

We do not sell personal information. Some external services may process data outside your country. Third-party links and services have their own policies.

Permissions and consent

The camera is used when you choose to take asset or document photos. Selected photos and documents are accessed through system pickers. You can decline camera permission and use existing files or manual entry. AI upload requires separate consent. Entered property coordinates are distinct from continuous device-location tracking; the app does not require background location access for its documented asset features.

Retention and account deletion

We retain active account records and saved assets while the account is used. You can initiate deletion from Profile → Delete Account or request deletion through our account-deletion page without reinstalling the app. Password accounts confirm their current password; social accounts reauthenticate with their original provider.

Deletion disables app sessions and removes personal assets, uploaded account documents, membership proofs, authored posts and comments, visitor invitations, memberships, bookings, and other account-linked records. Shared community documents uploaded by the account are removed; shared meeting records may remain with the organizer anonymized. Shared accounting records may remain with the app's account link, identifying metadata, and processor references removed. These anonymized records preserve community accounting totals.

Cleanup normally completes during the request. If a service fails, a durable deletion job retries automatically. Apple credentials are revoked before Apple account cleanup completes. A completion record without the account/content identifiers expires after 30 days. Support correspondence and external provider records may follow separate retention practices. If any additional identified record must be retained for a specific obligation, we will explain its purpose and applicable retention period when handling the request.

Security

Native app credentials are kept in iOS Keychain or Android Keystore-backed secure storage. Passwords are hashed. Production app builds require an HTTPS backend. Server-side access checks enforce account, membership, and administrator boundaries. No system can guarantee absolute security; report concerns to our support address.

Password recovery codes expire after 10 minutes and are limited to five attempts. Recovery emails are sent through our configured email provider. Resetting a password invalidates prior app sessions; recovery records are also removed on account deletion.

Your choices and children's privacy

You can edit asset records, decline optional permissions, revoke AI permission, report content, block community users, request account deletion, or contact support about your information. AuraInfra is not intended for children under 13. Do not submit children's personal information unless you have an appropriate basis and permission to do so.
