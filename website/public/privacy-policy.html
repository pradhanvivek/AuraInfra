AuraInfra Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

This Privacy Policy explains what information the AuraInfra mobile app ("AuraInfra", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. AuraInfra is a personal asset-management app that helps you catalog properties, vehicles, appliances, jewelry, furniture, and art.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: the username and password you choose, and optionally an email address, phone number, and profile photo.
  • Asset data you enter: names, descriptions, values, purchase dates, warranty information, and other details you add for the properties and items you track.
  • Photos: images you take with your camera or select from your photo library to attach to properties or other tracked assets.
  • Property addresses and coordinates: if you add a property address or use the map/nearby-places features, that address and its coordinates are stored and used to show nearby places of interest.

How We Use Third-Party Services

Some features send limited data to third-party services in order to work:

  • Google Maps / Places: property addresses and coordinates you provide may be sent to Google to power address autocomplete, geocoding, and the "nearby places" feature.
  • OpenStreetMap (Nominatim): used as a fallback to convert an address you enter into map coordinates.
  • AI-assisted features: optional features such as paint-cost estimation, Vastu/Feng Shui analysis, and photo-based asset recognition send the relevant photo or text you provide to a third-party AI provider in order to generate a result. We do not use this data to train third-party models beyond what is necessary to return your result.

How We Store and Protect Your Data

Your data is stored in our database and is only accessible to your account. Passwords are never stored in plain text — they are hashed before storage. Data is transmitted between the app and our servers over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your account and asset data for as long as your account is active. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from Profile → Delete Account in the app. This action is immediate and cannot be undone.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the third-party services described above, to the extent needed to provide the features you use, and where required by law.

Children's Privacy

AuraInfra is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Your Choices

  • You can edit or remove asset data and photos at any time within the app.
  • You can delete your account and all associated data from the Profile screen.
  • You can decline camera/photo-library permissions; features that rely on them simply won't be available.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at privacy@aurainfra.ai.
