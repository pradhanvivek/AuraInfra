Delete your AuraInfra account

Last updated: 3 October 2026

You can request deletion of your AuraInfra.ai account and associated personal data even if you have uninstalled the app.

Request deletion without the app

Email an account-deletion request

Email support@aurainfra.ai with the subject AuraInfra account deletion request. Include your registered username or account email and your sign-in method (password, Google, or Apple). For Apple private relay accounts, provide the relay email or username shown in your profile if available. Do not send your password, payment card details, or identity documents.

We verify that the request comes from the account owner before deleting data. Support will explain any additional verification needed and confirm the outcome. You do not need to reinstall the app to submit a request.

Delete directly in the app

  1. Open Profile → Delete Account.
  2. Confirm your current password, or reauthenticate with the Google/Apple account you originally used.
  3. Confirm permanent deletion. App access ends when the deletion request is accepted.

What is removed or retained

Your account, app sessions, personal asset records, photos, receipts, floor plans, uploaded documents, membership verification documents, authored community posts and comments, visitor invitations, membership links, and account-linked booking/complaint records are removed. Removing authored posts also removes their associated comments and likes.

Shared community accounting totals may remain with account identifiers, identifying metadata, and app-held processor references removed. Shared meetings may remain with an anonymized organizer. Payment/authentication providers may retain their own records under their policies. Completed deletion-job records no longer contain account/content identifiers and expire after 30 days.

If cleanup is temporarily unavailable, the app disables the account and queues deletion for automatic retry. Contact support with the request reference if you need help. Deletion cannot be undone.
