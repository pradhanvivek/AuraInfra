## Important Tips

### Smooth Navigation:
- **Tap slowly and deliberately** - Let animations complete
- **Pause 1-2 seconds** on each important screen
- **Avoid rapid scrolling** - Smooth, controlled movements
- **Don't rush** - Quality over speed

### What NOT to Show:
- ❌ Loading spinners (unless brief)
- ❌ Error messages
- ❌ Empty states
- ❌ Personal notifications
- ❌ Other apps or notifications

### Visual Focus:
- **Highlight key numbers:** Purchase cost, current value, warranty dates
- **Show badges clearly:** Warranty status indicators
- **Demonstrate features:** Actually open documents, view details
- **Natural flow:** Follow typical user journey

### Import:

2. Upload generated voiceover audio (all sections)

### Sync:
1. Descript auto-transcribes voiceover
2. Drag video clips to match voiceover timing
3. Trim video to match 2:30 target

### Enhancements:
- **Text Overlays** (optional):
  - "Track Multiple Properties" (0:20)
  - "AI-Powered Identification" (1:35)
  - "Never Miss Warranty Deadlines" (1:15)
  
- **Background Music:**
  - Low volume (10-15% of voiceover)

### Export:

- Format: MP4
- Frame rate: 30fps
