## PRODUCTION TIPS

### For Screen Recording:
1. **Set device to "Do Not Disturb"** - No interruptions
2. **Use consistent gestures** - Smooth taps and swipes
3. **Record in portrait mode** - Natural mobile experience
4. **Screen brightness at 80%** - Good visibility without glare
5. **Show natural pacing** - Don't rush through screens

### For Video Editing:
1. **Add subtle zoom effects** on key information (warranty dates, costs)
2. **Use smooth transitions** between sections (fade or slide)
3. **Add text overlays** for emphasis:
   - "Track Multiple Properties"
   - "AI-Powered Identification"
   - "Never Miss Warranty Deadlines"
4. **Background music:** Subtle, professional corporate track (low volume)
5. **End card:** Show logo + download buttons for 5 seconds

### For AI Voiceover:
**Recommended ElevenLabs Settings:**
- Voice: "Josh" (professional, trustworthy male) or "Rachel" (confident female)
- Stability: 60-70%
- Clarity: 70-80%
- Style: 0 (neutral, professional)
