<product_requirements>
The application is a comprehensive personal and property asset management system called AuraInfra.ai. It features a cross-platform Expo mobile app and a web-based admin dashboard.

**Core Functionality:**
- **User Management:** Users can register and log in. A recent update allows users to log in with either their email or username.
- **Asset Management:** Users can add, view, edit, and delete various assets, including properties, appliances, vehicles, and more.
- **AI-Powered Features:**
    - **Quick Scan:** Users can scan an item with their camera, and an AI service identifies the asset type (e.g., 'car', 'appliance') and navigates them to the appropriate add form. The scanned image and preliminary data are passed to the form to avoid redundant user input.
    - **Receipt Scanning:** Automatically fills in fixture details by scanning a receipt.
    - **Paint Estimation:** A tool to estimate paint quantity and cost by capturing room dimensions. This feature has been enhanced to support multiple countries (US, India, UK, etc.) with localized currency, measurement units, and region-specific pricing. It also includes a legal disclaimer.
- **Admin Dashboard:** A section of the app, also accessible via a separate web portal, for administrative tasks. The mobile admin dashboard header was fixed to display correctly on iPhones.
- **User Profile:** Users can manage their preferences, including a newly added global country setting that dictates default units and currencies throughout the app.
